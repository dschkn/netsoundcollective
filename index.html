01 / about

a laboratory
for new ideas

NetSound Collective grew out of an experimental project initiated by Dmitrii Shchukin during his 2023–2024 assistantship in the composition department at the Robert Schumann Hochschule Düsseldorf. What began as a study-driven ensemble gradually expanded into a full platform for experimentation, collaboration and public presentation.

As in an acoustic ensemble, each member of the laptop ensemble controls only one sound source. The performers are connected through a router, allowing signals and sound materials to be transmitted in real time. The result is a dynamic interdependent system in which every element reacts to the others.

For us, the laptop ensemble is not only a technical setup. It is a philosophical approach to music, centred on shared process, improvisation and the continuous transformation of material. The work values searching over closure: unexpected sound, unstable form and discovery in real time.

06 / Laptop+ · Call for Scores

LAPTOP+

Call for Scores

The ensemble is open to collaborations with composers, performers, media artists and interdisciplinary projects. We are interested in formats that explore new forms of sonic interaction, improvisation and performative practice.

Our Call for Scores invites composers and sound artists who want to test the boundaries between composition, improvisation and live processing, and to make specific use of the possibilities of a laptop ensemble.

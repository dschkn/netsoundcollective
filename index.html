01 / statement

NetSound Collective is more than an ensemble. It operates as a platform, a laboratory and a forum for contemporary electronic music, shaped by research in electronics, philosophy, music and transdigital aesthetics.

Performance becomes infrastructure: listening across distance, composing with unstable connections, translating gesture between acoustic space, code, image and live electronics.
